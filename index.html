A complete servo control loop running in your browser. You control only the lamp. An LDR measures its light, the ESP32 firmware turns that brightness into a servo angle and reads the position back, publishes the readings over MQTT, Node-RED stores them in InfluxDB, and the dashboard queries them with SQL. Three minutes of history were recorded when the page opened. Every stage has an editor, so change the code and watch the data change.
